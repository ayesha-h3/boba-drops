Cake Pops

How to make cake pops

  1. Make cake with flavor of your choice
  2. Buy or make your own frosting
  3. Put cake into stand mixer with attatchment
  4. Mix cake until crumbled into fine crumbs
  5. Add one tablespoon of your frosting into the crumbling cake, one tablespoon at a time until fully incorporated
  6. Add frosting into cake crumbs until forms into play dough consistency
  7. Roll cake mixture into 24 gram balls until you have no cake left
  8. Refrigerate cake balls for ten to fifteen minutes
  9. While cake is in the fridge, melt almond bark chocolate in microwave safe silicone bowl or glass cup
  10. Microwave for 15 second intervals, mixing well between intervals
  11. Keep microwaving for 15 second intervals until smooth
  12. Get cake pop sticks and dip top half into chocolate. Take cake balls out of fridge and put the chocolate covered part of the stick into it
  13. With a small spoon or extra cake pop stick, spread melting chocolate around the area where the two are connected, binding them so they don't fall while dipping
  14. Let sit for five to ten minutes while almond bark hardens
  15. After the almond bark sets, pick up the cake pop from the stick and carfully dip into melting chocolate in silicone or glass bowl or cup
  16. Dip until chocolate hits the stick and take out of chocolate. Tap stick on hand to let excess chocolate drip off
  17. Let cake pop sit in cake pop stand or foam while almond bark chocolate hardens. If desired, add sprinkles of decorations while chocolate is wet
  18. Repeat process with remaining cake pops until all cake pops are finsihed
  19. Enjoy!

Different ideas for cake pop decorating!

  • Edible glitter
  • Sprinkles
  • Different cololred chocolate drizzle
  • You could also try marbeling chocolate to get a different unique design for every cake pop!
